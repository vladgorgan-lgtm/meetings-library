# Community meetup: delivery practices

## Recall
Open session on how teams run retrospectives and track delivery health. Two practices stood out: a one-page health check per release, and rotating the facilitator.

## Decisions & actions
- **Decision:** Try the one-page release health check on the Platform team for two releases.
- [ ] **Jordan** - Share the health check template with the Platform team
- [x] **Nia** - Send the slides from the session

## Note
### Summary
Several teams described the same pain: retrospectives repeat the same findings. A short health check per release makes trends visible.

### Details
Nia presented the template: scope stability, defect escape rate, team load and one free-text risk. Tomas suggested rotating who facilitates so the same voices do not dominate.
